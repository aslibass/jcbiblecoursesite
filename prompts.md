Module 1: One-to-One Discipleship Training
(Grounded in the source: 121 PHILS TEXT.pdf)
1. Tailored Report Prompt (Leader's Curriculum Guide)
Core Purpose: To equip a spiritual facilitator with a comprehensive, lesson-by-lesson guide for conducting discipleship training
. Target Audience: Professional/Facilitators Prompt to Use: "Create a substantive, professional-level Training Report designed for a discipleship facilitator
. The report must outline the foundational steps of Christian discipleship
. Structure the document into three core sections: (1) **The Encounter**: explaining who Jesus is (His perfect humanity and deity)
, what He accomplished through His death, and what His current resurrected position means
; (2) **The Fellowship**: explaining how to teach the Daily Quiet Time (QT) using the **SPEAK method** (Sins to confess, Promises to keep, Examples to follow, Actions to take, Knowledge of God)
; and (3) **The Growth**: detailing the assurance of salvation using the **Train Diagram** (Fact, Faith, and Feeling)
 and handling temptation by categorizing attacks by source and strategy (Devil/Resist with Word, World/Examine devotional life, Flesh/Flee)
. Include practical guidelines on how a facilitator should spend quality time with a student to foster multiplication
."
2. Slide Deck Prompt (Course Overview Presentation)
Visual Style: ethereal_gradient or arch_tech_minimalism Prompt to Use: "Design a standard, reading-style presentation deck to introduce students to the 'One-to-One Discipleship' journey
. Establish a clear, structured sequence across these slides: (1) **Introduction to Spiritual Multiplication**: outlining the mandate of Matthew 28:19-20
; (2) **Who is Jesus?**: introducing His identity as both perfect human and perfect God
; (3) **The Quiet Time (QT) Blueprint**: a step-by-step practical guide on preparing the mind, meditating on the Word, and documenting insights in a QT notebook
; (4) **The Mechanics of Salvation**: outlining the intellectual, emotional, and willful commitments of faith
; and (5) **The Five Elements of Prayer**: introducing Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, Intercession, and Supplication
. Use clear, high-contrast typography and a clean layout suitable for classroom teaching
."
3. Infographic Prompt (The Anatomy of Discipleship)
Visual Style: sketch_note or bento_grid Prompt to Use: "Generate a bento-grid style infographic that maps out the practical spiritual habits of a growing Christian
. Create distinct visual sections for: (1) **The Word Intake Hand**: visually representing how Hearing, Reading, Studying, Memorizing, and Meditating work together to gain a firm grasp of Scripture
; (2) **The Train of Assurance**: illustrating the correct order of Fact (the engine), Faith (the fuel/firebox), and Feeling (the caboose)
; and (3) **The Temptation Grid**: displaying the three channels of spiritual attack (Devil, World, Flesh) and their corresponding defensive strategies (Resist, Examine, Flee)
. Ensure the style is highly structured, modern, and easy to memorize
."
4. Video Overview Prompt (Course Launch Video)
Format Preset: explainer (animated narrated slides) Prompt to Use: "Create an animated explainer video script introducing the 'One-to-One Discipleship Training' course
. Focus the narrative on the vision of mutual growth and spiritual multiplication
. The video should explain that discipleship is not merely about transferring intellectual knowledge, but about committing the will, sharing life experiences, and walking together with a facilitator
. Walk through the transition from initial 'Encounter' to deep personal 'Fellowship' (Quiet Time) and eventual 'Growth' in attributes like prayer, obedience, and active church ministry
. Maintain a warm, encouraging, and highly motivational tone."
Module 2: Christian Life
(Grounded in the source: CL TEXT PHILS.pdf)
1. Tailored Report Prompt (Spiritual Life Inventory Manual)
Core Purpose: To provide a comprehensive diagnostic evaluation manual for measuring spiritual maturity
. Target Audience: Professional/Counselors Prompt to Use: "Create a highly detailed, professional-grade diagnostic report that serves as a 'Spiritual Life Inventory'
. The report must detail: (1) **The Spiritual Anatomy**: comparing the Natural Person, Spiritual Person, Infant Christian, and Carnal Christian
; (2) **The Role of Grace & The Spirit**: detailing how God's grace provides both the gift of salvation and the enabling power for sanctification
, and explaining the three impacts of the Holy Spirit (Christ-awareness, transformation into His likeness, and inner assurance of salvation)
; (3) **The 14 Traits of Godly Character**: providing summary definitions and developmental strategies for humility, self-control, patience, meekness, and brotherly love
; and (4) **Guidance and Decision Making**: listing the six ways God communicates (Bible, Direct Revelation, Providence, Wise Counsel, Illumination, Gifted Christians)
 and the two essential conditions for receiving it (Obedience and Humility)
."
2. Slide Deck Prompt (Priorities & Time Management Presentation)

3. Infographic Prompt (The Believer's Spiritual Blueprint)

Module 3: Galatians: Justification by Faith and Christian Liberty
(Grounded in the source: GALATIANS PHILS TEXT.pdf)
1. Tailored Report Prompt (The Doctrinal Crisis of Legalism)
Core Purpose: To provide a comprehensive theological report examining the historical and contemporary crisis of legalism
. Target Audience: Professional/Theologians Prompt to Use: "Create an in-depth, professional Theological Report analyzing the epistle to the Galatians
. The report must be divided into three parts matching Paul's outline: (1) **The Historical Argument (Chapters 1-2)**: detailing Paul's defense of his divine apostolic calling
, the transition of the early church from Judaism to Christianity (the four historical steps)
, and his public correction of Peter's hypocrisy
; (2) **The Theological Argument (Chapters 3-4)**: exploring the doctrine of justification by faith alone
, the historical example of Abraham
, the purpose of the Law as a temporary schoolmaster
, and the allegory of Hagar and Sarah representing the two covenants
; and (3) **The Practical Argument (Chapters 5-6)**: comparing circumcision versus faith
, detailing the internal war between the Flesh and the Spirit
, listing the works of the flesh and the corresponding fruit of the Spirit
, and explaining the harvest principle (reaping what we sow)
."
2. Slide Deck Prompt (The Righteous Shall Live by Faith)
Visual Style: conceptual_tech_thesis or retro_print Prompt to Use: "Design a highly academic and visual reading-style slide deck that captures the core themes of the book of Galatians
. Establish a slide flow that covers: (1) **The Core Theme**: explaining Galatians 3:11 and the historical impact of the Protestant Reformation
; (2) **The Galatian Crisis**: explaining who the Judaizers were and their false gospel of 'Faith + Works'
; (3) **The Transition Timeline**: mapping out the early church’s transition from Jerusalem to Antioch of Syria
; (4) **Circumcision and Gospel Freedom**: explaining why Paul circumcised Timothy but refused to circumcise Titus, illustrating his principle of putting the gospel first
; (5) **The Allegory of Two Covenants**: Sarah vs. Hagar, freedom vs. slavery
; and (6) **Life in the Spirit**: visualizing the Fruit of the Spirit as a supernatural tree growing from the indwelling Holy Spirit
."
3. Infographic Prompt (Ritual vs. Reality: The Galatians Visual)
Visual Style: storyboard or professional Prompt to Use: "Generate a professional infographic illustrating the stark contrasts between Ceremonial Religion and Spiritual Experience as outlined in Galatians
. Divide the layout into two halves: Left Column: **Ceremonial Religion/The Law** (symbolized by a yoke of slavery
, a speed-limit sign representing external restraint
, and Hagar/bondage
); Right Column: **Spiritual Experience/The Gospel** (symbolized by the Fruit of the Spirit tree
, the freedom of a grown son/heir
, and Sarah/promise
). Add a central cross highlighting Galatians 2:20 as the point of transition
. Ensure the layout is clean, text-minimal, and highly symbolic."
4. Video Overview Prompt (The Liberty of Sonship Explainer)
Format Preset: explainer Prompt to Use: "Create a highly engaging video script explaining 'The Gospel of Christian Liberty' based on Galatians 4
. The video should use the analogy of a child under guardians and managers who is treated no better than a slave until he comes of age
. Explain how Jesus was born under the law to redeem those under the law, giving us the full rights of grown sons
. Contrast the external motivation of rules with the internal motivation of the Holy Spirit crying 'Abba, Father!' in our hearts
. Conclude with a strong, passionate plea to 'stand fast' in the freedom Christ has given and not submit again to a yoke of slavery
."
