Course Introduction
### DESCRIPTION OF THE COURSE

In this course you will study spiritual growth. You will look at what it is
and how it is attained. The lesson materials focus on what you need in
order to see progress in your spiritual life. Only those who have
experienced the regenerating work of God in their lives have the ability
to realize the goal of spiritual maturity. It is in the saving work of God
that the believers receive all that they need for living the Christian life.
While it is beyond the scope of this course to do a detailed study
of the doctrine of regeneration, it would be helpful for you to consider
some of the benefits of your salvation that make it possible for you to
grow spiritually. May the Lord enrich your life as you study.
### OBJECTIVES OF THE COURSE

**1. Define the goal and the process for achieving spiritual growth.**

**2. Define spiritual maturity and explain the three major principles**

that influence it.
**3. Identify fourteen godly characteristics that will be evident as a**

result of spiritual growth, and apply them to your life.
**4. Explain how each of the following contributes to spiritual**

growth: Bible study, prayer, and faith.
**5. Explain the seven principles for making wise decisions.**

**6. Create a list of life's goals, arrange those goals in order of**

priority and develop a plan of action for implementing those
activities.
**7. Define the five reasons for believers' sufferings.**

**8. Identify the two fundamental ministry responsibilities all**

believers have for one another.
**9. Identify the two fundamental ministries that all believers have to**

non-believers.
**10. Describe the six pieces of the believer's “armor of God,” their**

spiritual significance, and their practical value.

of a fleece involved miraculous power and was used in accompaniment
with a known principle or established directive from God's word, this
method cannot be recommended as a general way of gaining guidance.

#### A. Recognizing the Enemy

We are in a battle between God and Satan. Our association with God
pits us against the forces of Satan in this spiritual war. You must
properly assess your own resources for the battle. In this we find hope,
for we fight not from weakness or retreat but from the victory already
won and assured by the death and resurrection of Jesus Christ. Because
of this, we need not fear our foe, only recognize his strength and
deceitfulness, and then plan for his attacks.
**1. The Schemes of Satan**

We must know Satan's scheme to fight against him. Satan and his army
are limited in the use of their power but unlimited in the use of their
intelligence and wisdom. Therefore, they come not as evil rulers but as
deceitful workers, angels of light (2 Cor. 11:13, 14).
a. Glance of the History
Objective 1: Understand the history of human being in regard to Satan
and sin.
In the beginning mankind was without sin. Adam only knew the
blessings of God and the prohibition of the tree of the knowledge of
good and evil. Set before him was life and blessing, death and cursing.
The only qualification for life and blessing was to believe God (trust)
and obey.
And Satan enters through the serpent and sets before man the lie
that the creature does not have to be responsible to the Creator in order
to receive the blessings of God; that independence from God is a viable
option in God's universe; that peace, joy, and meaning to life can be
achieved outside of God and without penalty when, in reality, these
things are only obtained in dependence on him (Deut. 30:15-20).
So Eve took and ate and gave to her husband (Gen. 3:6). The
woman was deceived, but the man was not. He had openly rebelled (1
Tim. 2:14). Sin was therefore introduced through Adam (Rom. 5:12).

No Roman soldier in his right mind would ever go into battle without

accusations of Satan are avoided in the righteousness of Christ, his
imputed righteousness.
